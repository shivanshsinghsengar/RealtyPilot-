SLIDE 5: KEY FEATURES EXPLAINED
====================================================

Title: How RealtyPilot Helps Your Daily Operations

FEATURE 1: AI-Based Lead Scoring

The system automatically calculates a score (0-100) for each lead:

Budget Alignment (25 points)
• Matches customer budget with your projects
• Higher budget = Higher score
• Budget confirmed = Extra points

Timeline Urgency (20 points)
• Buying within 1 month = Maximum points
• Next 3 months = Medium points
• Future planning = Lower points

Engagement Level (20 points)
• Responds quickly = Higher score
• Asks detailed questions = Shows interest
• Opens shared brochures = Engagement indicator
• Multiple interactions = More points

Site Visit Progress (35 points)
• Visit scheduled = 15 bonus points
• Visit completed = 35 bonus points

Score Interpretation:
• 85-100: HOT - Contact immediately
• 70-84: WARM - Follow up this week
• 50-69: COLD - Regular monthly follow-up
• Below 50: Nurture with periodic updates

FEATURE 2: 30-Second Site Visit Scheduling

Traditional Way:
1. Note down customer request
2. Check calendar
3. Call customer back
4. Send location on WhatsApp
5. Set reminder
6. Risk of forgetting

With RealtyPilot:
1. Click "Schedule Site Visit"
2. Select project from dropdown
3. Choose date and time
4. Click Confirm
✓ Automatic WhatsApp confirmation sent
✓ Reminder set automatically
✓ Appears in team calendar
✓ All details saved

FEATURE 3: Call Notes & Conversation History

After Every Call:
• Click "Add Call Note" button
• Type what customer said
• Save
• Automatically timestamped
• Entire team can see it
• Complete timeline maintained

Example Timeline:
Today, 3:45 PM - Call Note
"Customer wants east-facing plot. Budget increased to 35L. Wants to visit this Saturday."

Yesterday, 11:20 AM - WhatsApp Message
"Can you send more photos of Narmada Greens?"

"First call. Interested in 1000 sq ft plot. Loan needed."
